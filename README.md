One absurd clause buried deep in a terms-of-service agreement: when the agreement ends, the User's shadow stays the Company's property — licensed, sold, or displayed in the Company lobby.

> Upon termination, the User's shadow shall remain the exclusive property of the Company and may be licensed, sold, or displayed in the Company lobby at its sole discretion.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the clause to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one sentence, phrased as deadpan legal boilerplate.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
